# Glossary

Term · plain explanation · the wording the page and the e-mail use.
Decisions behind the wording are in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

## Terms

| Term | Plain explanation | Czech wording |
|---|---|---|
| ETF | A fund traded on an exchange like a share; one purchase buys a small piece of many companies. | ETF, fond |
| Index | A list of companies with rules for how much of each to hold; the fund copies it. | index |
| S&P 500 | 503 US companies (index factsheet, 30 Sep 2026). | S&P 500 |
| Nasdaq-100 | 100 companies listed on Nasdaq, 68 % technology (index factsheet, 30 Sep 2026). | Nasdaq-100 |
| MSCI World | 1 249 companies from several countries, 72.94 % of it US (index factsheet, 30 Sep 2026). Which countries count is not in the research yet; check before the page describes it. | Svět (MSCI World) |
| NYSE | The New York Stock Exchange. VOO, SPY, IVV and URTH are listed on NYSE Arca, QQQ on Nasdaq (SEC prospectuses, see research/pairs.md). | NYSE; the QQQ row says "(Nasdaq)" |
| US ETF | A fund registered in the US, e.g. VOO, SPY, IVV, QQQ, URTH. | ETF z NYSE, americké ETF |
| EU fund (UCITS ETF) | A fund registered in the EU (here Ireland) that EU brokers may sell to retail investors. "UCITS" is not used in explanatory copy; it appears only inside a fund's full name. | evropský fond; in ad B "evropský protějšek" |
| KID | The key information document EU rules require before a fund is sold to a retail investor. US issuers do not publish one. | dokument KID (klíčové informace) |
| Same index | The EU fund copies the same index as the US fund it replaces. | stejný index |
| ISIN | A 12-character code that identifies one fund everywhere; the way to find it in a broker's app. | ISIN |
| TER | The fund's yearly cost, taken from the fund's value, not billed. | poplatek fondu (TER), % ročně |
| Broker | The app or firm through which you buy the fund. | broker |
| Currency-conversion fee | What the broker charges to change koruna into the fund's currency at each purchase. | směna měny |
| Commission | A fee per order. | provize |
| Accumulating (Acc) | The fund reinvests dividends itself; nothing is paid out. | akumulační |
| Distributing (Dist) | The fund pays dividends out; they are taxed. | distribuční |
| Return | How much the investment grew, including reinvested dividends. Not interest: it is not guaranteed and can be negative. | výnos (never "úrok") |
| 10-year average return | The yearly rate that, applied every year, gives the same 10-year result. Actual years were higher and lower. | průměrně … % ročně za 10 let |
| Total return, net / gross | Return including dividends; "net" after withholding tax on them, "gross" before. Shown only in source notes. | (source notes only) |
| Income illustration | The monthly amount grown for 10 years at the index's 10-year average; a historical statistic, not a forecast. | Kolik by z toho mohlo být |
| Time test | A sale is tax-free if the fund was held more than 3 years. | držíte déle než 3 roky |
| 100 000 Kč limit | A sale is tax-free if total sale proceeds in the year are at most 100 000 Kč. | prodáte za max. 100 000 Kč za rok |
| Tax on gains | Otherwise the gain is taxed at 15 %, or 23 % above the yearly threshold. | daň ze zisku 15 % (23 %) |
| Data controller | Who is responsible for the e-mail address under GDPR. | správce údajů |

## Words to avoid

| Avoid | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| dvojče (twin) | Decided in C2. | evropský fond, stejný index |
| zakázané, nelegální (banned, illegal) | Not supported: the limit comes from the missing KID, not a ban. | většinou nekoupíte, většina brokerů je nenabízí |
| úrok (interest) | Returns are not guaranteed interest. | výnos |
| doporučujeme, nejlepší fond (we recommend, the best fund) | The page is not investment advice. | fond, který sleduje stejný index |
| UCITS, PRIIPs, ICAV | Jargon a beginner does not need; source notes only. | evropský fond, dokument KID |
