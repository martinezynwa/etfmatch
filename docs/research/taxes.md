Collected and re-checked against the live sources on 2026-10-15. Overview and scope: [README](README.md).

Scope: Czech tax-resident individual (not a business), buying and selling UCITS ETFs through a foreign broker, holding them outside business assets (outside "obchodní majetek").

## Not verified / interpretation

1. **Interpretation:** That a UCITS ETF share or unit (for example an Irish ICAV or plc share) is a "cenný papír" for § 4 odst. 1 písm. t)/u) and § 10. The law has no ETF-specific wording. This is the generally used reading, but I found no FS text that names ETFs.
2. **Interpretation:** Whether the 100 000 Kč test in písm. t) counts all gross sale proceeds, including sales already exempt under the 3-year test. The wording "úhrn" (total) suggests all proceeds count. Secondary sources (pohoda.cz, money.cz) say so. Not confirmed by an FS text I found.
3. **Interpretation:** How to identify which units were sold when the same ETF was bought several times (FIFO, average price or specific lots). ZDP has no explicit rule. Not verified.
4. **Interpretation:** Currency conversion of foreign-currency proceeds and costs (§ 38 ZDP, single or annual exchange rate). Not researched. Not verified.
5. **Interpretation:** Whether the 50 000 Kč (§ 38g odst. 1) and 20 000 Kč (§ 38g odst. 2) return thresholds compare gross § 10 income or income after expenses. The law says "příjmy" (income). I did not find the official FS reading, so this needs a tax advisor or an FS source.
6. **Interpretation:** For § 38v, whether "jednotlivý příjem" (single income) means each sell order or each settlement. FS (2016) says only that each income is assessed separately.
7. **Interpretation:** Whether a dividend from an Irish ETF counts as "podíly na zisku obchodní korporace nebo podílového fondu" under § 8 odst. 1 písm. a). Also any Irish withholding tax and its credit under § 38f or the treaty. Not researched.
8. **Not verified:** Popular sources say the 100 000 Kč test applied "from 2014". The primary text I found shows it introduced by 267/2014 Sb., effective 1 Jan 2015. The transitional provisions I read do not allow it for 2014 (bod 4 allowing early use concerns písm. u, which was housing). ZOS 344/2013 Sb. contains no 100 000 Kč securities test. Reported as 2015.
