⏺ I found 4 bugs that affect what the visitor gets, plus a set of copy, naming
  and visual inconsistencies. I went through all of /, every tap combination,
  all accordions open, every e-mail form state (empty, invalid, valid), the
  modal, the screen after sending, and /ochrana-udaju. The browser window
  wouldn't resize, so I checked mobile in a 375 px frame on the same site. Its
  scrollbar leaves about 360 px of content. I didn't check facts or figures.

  Bugs (fix first)

  1. The modal scrolls sideways on mobile. The first table in the e-mail ("Kolik
     by z toho mohlo být") is 379 px wide inside a 322 px dialog. The "Svět"
     column is cut off and the whole dialog scrolls left and right. The second
     table wraps fine, so the two don't match. Wrap the first table in
     overflow-x-auto or let its cells wrap.
  2. The page behind the modal scrolls. Once the modal reaches its end, further
     scrolling moves the page underneath (it went from the ask block to the
     FAQ). Nothing locks the page scroll and the dialog has no
     overscroll-behavior: contain.
  3. Copying the ISIN copies the footnote too. A footnote number sits right
     after the ISIN, so copying gives IE00B4L5Y983 plus a 1. Pasting the ISIN
     into the broker app is the main thing the visitor needs to do. Move the
     footnote off the ISIN or add a copy button.
  4. The screen after sending goes stale. If I change the index after sending,
     "Hotovo" stays, and "Zobrazit e-mail znovu" now shows a plan for the new
     index. That is not the plan that was "sent". The heading "Pošleme vám celý
     plán…" and the checklist also stay up after sending.
  5. E-mail error state. On an empty submit the field gets no error styling; the
     only red border is the focus ring. Focus stays on the button instead of
     moving to the field. The old error ("Vyplňte e-mail…") stays visible while
     typing until the next submit.

  Grammar and copy (src/copy/cs.ts)

  6. "Pošleme vám celý plán k Svět (MSCI World)" is the wrong case, and "k
     Nasdaq-100" reads oddly too. Suggest "…celý plán pro index {X}".
  7. Footer: "Broker je uveden…" The page shows two brokers, so it should be
     "Brokeři jsou uvedeni jen jako místo, kde fond najdete…".
  8. Modal "Podíl USA: neuvádí" has no subject. Use "neuvedeno". Also "68 %
     technologie" should be "68 % v technologiích".
  9. Modal note: "I fond „Svět“ má … peněz v USA". Everywhere else Svět is an
     index, not a fund.
  10. Subject line "Předmět: Váš ETF plán: Svět, 5 000 Kč…" has two colons, and
      uses bare "Svět" while the body says "Svět (MSCI World)".
  11. "Za nákup za 5 000 Kč, pokud máte účet v Kč." is a fragment with "za…za"
      and no verb. It explains the broker cards but sits below them.
  12. "Hotovo, takhle plán vypadá." shows after the modal is closed, so "takhle"
      points at nothing.
  13. Checklist "Daně v Česku stručně: 3 roky, 100 000 Kč, 15 %" gives a
      beginner bare numbers with no context.
  14. Commission is worded three ways: "Provize … je u obou 0 Kč" on the page,
      then "nákup bez provize" and "provize 0 % do 100 000 € měsíčně" in the
      modal. € also appears in a Kč-only interface.
  15. Same footnote twice: footnote 7 appears twice on one line in the modal's
      fees section.

  Names that don't match

  16. Index name: the button says "Svět", headings say "Svět (MSCI World)", the
      e-mail subject says "Svět".
  17. Fund names:
      - "iShares NASDAQ 100" in capitals, while the index is "Nasdaq-100"
        everywhere else.
      - Only the Nasdaq fund lacks "(Acc)".
      - The first-screen column is headed "NYSE", but the QQQ row says it trades
        on Nasdaq.
  18. Number of top companies: the page shows the top 5 ("Největší firmy
      uvnitř"); the e-mail shows "Největší 3".
  19. Number formats: the modal mixes "cca 723 000 Kč" and "cca 1,20 mil. Kč" in
      the same row.

  Visual patterns and styling

  20. The two tap rows look different.
      - Index buttons are as wide as their text, at 15 px.
      - Amount buttons sit in an equal-width grid, at 14 px.
  21. The +/× icons are misaligned. In globals.css they're placed at a fixed
      top: 6px or 20px instead of being centred:
      - FAQ "+" sits below the text on one-line questions and centred on
        two-line ones.
      - The sources "+" sits above the text.
      - The sizes differ (20 vs 22 px).
  22. No type scale.
      - Headings are 18, 16, 26 and 20 px.
      - The "Evropský fond místo…" label is a heading at 16 px, while the bigger
        fund name below it is not a heading.
      - Components use 29 hard-coded font sizes (about 11 distinct ones) and
        weights 600, 700, 750, 800, 850 and 900.
      - Colours come from @theme tokens; font sizes and weights don't.
  23. Red means too many things. It is used for:
      - buttons
      - errors
      - the focus ring
      - warning boxes
      - the Nvidia bar
      - the selected column in the modal (with the same pink background as the
        warnings)
      - the checklist ticks

      The modal's close button opens with a red focus ring, which looks like an
      error.
  24. Things that look tappable but aren't:
      - The red filled ticks in the checklist look like checked checkboxes.
      - The bordered broker cards look like buttons but aren't links.
  25. Mobile wrapping:
      - "směna měny 7,50 Kč" breaks, leaving the amount alone on a line, and the
        two broker cards end up uneven.
      - The headline takes 5 lines with "ano." alone at the end; text-wrap: 
        balance would help.
  26. Small tap targets:
      - The "Zdroje dat" and "Zdroje" toggles are 36 px tall.
      - The links "Jak s e-mailem zacházíme" and "Ochrana osobních údajů" are
        12.5 px text, 14 px tall.
      - The consent text is 12.5 px grey on black.
  27. "Jak s e-mailem zacházíme" leaves the page mid-form, in the same tab,
      which drops the visitor out of the funnel.
  28. Modal reading:
      - There is no close button or next step at the bottom; you have to scroll
        back up to ×.
      - The note under the first table is a 7-sentence block of 13 px grey text.
  29. Minor:
      - The privacy page only has its back link at the bottom.
      - On desktop the modal (560 px) is wider than the page column (448 px).

  - Header bar: 52 → 44 px, and no gap above the headline.
  - Table rows: the padding goes from 20 to 14 px per row.

    button shrinks by about 4 px each.

  That makes the button fully visible once Safari's bars shrink. With the bars
  expanded it is still about 28 px short. Spacing alone can't close that gap
  without cutting content, which point 2 handles differently.

  2. Keeping the next step in view

  What a visitor does now on a 548 px screen:

  1. Tap the first button. The page scrolls to the index and amount taps.
  2. Scroll about 1,100 px through the fund card to "Chci vědět víc ↓", with no

     "Poslat plán" is below the screen.

  visible.

  plus a reordered e-mail block.

    - On the first screen it reads "Najít evropský fond ↓" and replaces the

    button does its job. That saves 72 px.
